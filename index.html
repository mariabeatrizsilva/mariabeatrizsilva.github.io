Hi! I'm Maria Beatriz, a Master's in Visual Computing and Creative AI at École Polytechnique. My research sits at the intersection of graphics, machine learning and human perception, and I want to build visual tools that speed up scientific discovery and help the people who need them.

Previously, I graduated summa cum laude from NYU Courant with a B.S. in Computer Science (honors) and a minor in Mathematics. There, I worked with Prof. Qi Sun at the Immersive Computing Lab on perceptual rendering and on evaluating AI-generated video, and helped build PaleoScan, a low-cost 3D scanner for fossil collections. I've also built internal tools for designers and animators at Duolingo.

I'm grateful to be supported by a Fulbright Open Study/Research Award. In my free time, I enjoy running and visiting museums, and I share my year abroad with K-12 classrooms through Reach the World (see my journey here).
